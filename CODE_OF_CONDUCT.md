# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project spaces must remain respectful, technically focused, and welcoming. Constructive disagreement is acceptable; harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and publication of private server/player data are not.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact channels when public discussion would expose a person or sensitive information.

---

# 行为准则

项目交流必须保持尊重、专注技术并欢迎参与。允许建设性分歧；禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击、公开个人信息、垃圾内容以及发布服务器或玩家隐私数据。

维护者可编辑、隐藏或删除违反准则的内容，并可临时或永久限制参与。若公开讨论会暴露个人或敏感信息，请通过仓库安全/联系渠道私下报告。
